# Bilt 2027 calculation assumptions

Verified October 31, 2026 against Bilt's October 27 announcement and program overview:
- https://newsroom.biltrewards.com/2027-bilt-program-updates
- https://support.biltrewards.com/hc/en-us/articles/42897766682381-Bilt-Card-2-0-Program-Overview
- https://www.biltrewards.com/terms/bilt-card-offer-terms

Select the benefit period manually. New accounts from January 1, 2027 use refreshed benefits. Earlier accounts retain their current structure until their first anniversary in 2027. Eligible Flexible users must opt out before that anniversary to retain Flexible and eligible existing credits; that election prevents mode switching. The calculator does not submit an election or infer eligibility from dates. Product changes and partial transition years require checking the account's actual terms.

Annual optimizer housing estimates assume twelve equal monthly housing payments and equal monthly everyday spending. A below-25% spend ratio earns 250 points per paid billing cycle under Housing-only. At qualifying tiers use the stated multiplier, not a universal minimum. No housing payment earns no housing points. Flexible uses 4% spending-generated cash at $0.03 per housing point up to 1x housing; unused cash has no assigned value. Monthly Deep Dive assumes 1x everyday purchase points, so it is illustrative rather than a card-specific total. Crossovers are sampled, not exact thresholds.

Refreshed Obsidian receives $100 annual Bilt Cash and Palladium $600, replacing the prior hotel/cash benefits, on the account anniversary. These are restricted credits, not cash back. Separate usable-dollar inputs now value refreshed and eligible legacy credits, capped at $100 for Obsidian and $600 for Palladium, defaulting to zero. These values affect ranking before top results are selected and are included in single-card comparisons and displayed totals. Enter only savings you can realize after restrictions and expiration, excluding cash already used for housing points. Legacy hotel credits require eligible bookings; the combined Palladium input represents eligible cash and hotel benefits, not unrestricted cash. Hotel cash redemption allowances consume existing cash and are not additional credits. Bilt Cash expires at end-of-month twelve months after earning. Daily Rewards, status, milestone cash, Point Accelerator, unused spending-generated cash and cash balances are not valued.

Automatic selection and its internal branches have been removed. Refreshed benefits force Housing-only; grandfathered benefits force Flexible. In transition-year mode, enter complete billing cycles before the anniversary (0–12) and select the pre-transition mode. The remainder use Housing-only. Annual housing value is the cycle-weighted sum of each mode's annual value, assuming equal monthly spend and rent. For example six Flexible cycles and six Housing-only cycles receive half of each annual value. Assign an anniversary-straddling cycle according to the actual statement; exact-day proration is not claimed.

Transition-year credit inputs are actual usable amounts from deposits in each period and are added without monthly proration. Enter zero for benefits not received or not usable; do not enter the same benefit twice. These inputs do not predict January legacy deposits, anniversary adjustments, or product-change treatment. Fees remain full annual fees. This is a twelve-cycle estimate, not a daily cash-flow forecast. Apply commits all inputs together; Clear restores conservative defaults. Portfolios include at most one Bilt card so spend thresholds and annual credits are not pooled across incompatible Bilt products. Deep Dive remains a monthly hypothetical A/B comparison, independent of the annual transition blend and annual credits.
